# Audio assets

Place legally obtained audio files here or in the project root. Exact filenames are preferred, but the app also recognizes the artist and title when their order is swapped or separators differ, such as `Experience - Ludovico Einaudi.ogg`.

- `Ludovico Einaudi - Experience.mp3`
- `Kaleo - Way Down We Go.mp3`
- `Royal Deluxe - Dangerous.mp3`
- `OneRepublic - Run.mp3`
- `Shawn Mendes - Stitches.mp3`

The app has generated WAV fallback effects for dealing, flipping, winning, and losing.
